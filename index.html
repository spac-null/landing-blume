SENSORY TRANSLATION LAB

I translate silence into images, spaces, and tools.

I am a Deaf artist and designer based between Amsterdam and Bologna. I make films, photographs, installations, and accessibility systems. The medium changes. The question stays the same: what happens when you build culture from a different sensory starting point?

Work shown at Van Abbemuseum, Eindhoven. Featured at IDFA. Supported by Creative Europe and Comune di Bologna.

CULTURAL TENSIONS

Accessibility is not an afterthought.
It is a design philosophy.

01

Institutions design for the majority, then retrofit for the rest.

Museums, festivals, and cultural spaces treat accessibility as compliance — checkbox accommodations bolted onto finished products. This approach misses something fundamental: accessibility constraints often generate the most innovative design solutions.

02

Deaf perception exposes blind spots in dominant systems.

When you build culture primarily through sound, you miss the visual frequencies, spatial rhythms, and tactile signals that shape how millions experience the world. Deafness is not absence — it is a different sensory hierarchy that reveals what hearing-centric design overlooks.

03

Translation is not simplification. It is transformation.

Every cultural and sensory translation changes meaning. Rather than treating this as loss, I work with translation as a creative force — shifting between Dutch, Italian, Sign Language, and visual systems to generate new forms of knowing.

PRACTICE

Five disciplines,
one underlying question

[01]

Film & Documentary

Feature films and short documentaries about Deaf life, sign language identity, and sensory experience. Deaf-led storytelling where the camera follows a different logic than hearing-centric narrative.

[02]

Photography

Series-based portraiture and documentary work. Intimate, long-duration projects about aging, identity, and how people inhabit their bodies over time.

[03]

Installation & Video

Multi-channel video installations and spatial work that translate between senses. Environments built to be experienced through sight and touch, not only sound.

[04]

Accessibility Design

Consulting and building with institutions on accessibility systems, sign language interfaces, and inclusive cultural programming. Accessibility as a design principle, not a retrofit.

[05]

Social Enterprise

Co-founding and running cultural spaces where radical inclusion is the business model. Proving that disability employment and financial sustainability are not contradictions.

I SIGN, I LIVE
2012 — FEATURE DOCUMENTARY

I Sign, I Live

A feature-length documentary following Deaf individuals across cultures, exploring how sign language shapes identity, community, and the experience of the world. The film treats sign language not as accommodation but as a complete linguistic and cultural system. Shown at Van Abbemuseum and international festivals.

FILM SIGN LANGUAGE IDENTITY
KALOKAGATHIA
2020 — VIDEO PORTRAIT SERIES

Kalokagathia

Nine collaborators — Bart, Casper, Kim, Maaike, Marie, Pieternel, Sif, Stella, and Marq — filmed in intimate video portraits that explore the ancient Greek ideal of beauty and goodness in harmony. Each portrait asks: what does it mean to embody both inner and outer wholeness? Multi-channel video installation.

ELDERLY
2020 — PHOTOGRAPHY, 8 WORKS

Elderly

A photographic series that refuses to look away from aging. Where culture marginalizes older people or renders them invisible, these eight portraits look closely, with respect and without sentimentality. Documentary portraiture as an act of care.

PHOTOGRAPHY PORTRAITURE AGING
BINNENKAMER
2020 — INSTALLATION

Binnenkamer

An installation exploring the Dutch concept of the "binnenkamer" — the inner room — as both physical location and psychological state. The work plays with where private space ends and public space begins, and what it means to invite someone inside.

INSTALLATION SPACE DUTCH CULTURE
ME_MYSELF
2011–2020 — PHOTOGRAPHY

Me_Myself

A decade of self-documentation. Not vanity but inquiry: what does it mean to witness yourself over ten years? Identity as process, not state. The archive itself becomes a form of research into how a person changes and persists.

L'ALTRO SPAZIO
ONGOING — SOCIAL COOPERATIVE, BOLOGNA

L'Altro Spazio

A cultural bar and event space in Bologna, co-founded as a social cooperative. Eighty percent of staff come from vulnerable backgrounds. The project tests a model where radical inclusion and financial sustainability reinforce each other rather than compete.

COOPERATIVE CULTURAL SPACE BOLOGNA
TOLK / TOLKNET
2020 — MIXED MEDIA

Tolk / Tolknet

A project that treats interpretation and translation not as neutral service but as artistic material. "Tolk" is Dutch for interpreter — someone who stands between languages and cultures. The work examines what happens in the space between what is said and what is understood.
